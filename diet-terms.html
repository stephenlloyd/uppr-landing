Terms of Use — uppr diet

Last updated: 2 July 2026

What uppr diet is

uppr diet is a nutrition and weight-management tracking app. It helps you log food, track calories and macros, follow your weight trend, and keep a personal record of things you choose to track — including fasting, hydration and medication schedules.

Not medical advice

uppr diet is a tracking tool, not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical, dietary or pharmaceutical advice. In particular:

  • Calorie and macro targets are informational estimates based on standard formulas and your own logged data.
  • The medication features only record and remind you of a plan that comes from your prescriber. The app never recommends, calculates or adjusts doses.
  • Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before changing your diet, starting or stopping a medication, or acting on anything the app shows you — especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or a history of disordered eating.

Your data, your responsibility

The accuracy of what the app shows depends on what you log. Nutrition data from public databases and label scanning may contain errors — check anything you rely on. Community-contributed foods are provided as-is.

Acceptable use

Don't submit deliberately false data to the community food database, attempt to access other users' data, or use the service in a way that breaks the law or disrupts the service for others.
