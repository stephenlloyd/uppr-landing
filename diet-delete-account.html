Delete your uppr diet account

uppr diet · developed by uppr fit · Last updated: 2 July 2026

How to request deletion of your account

  1. Email support@uppr.fit with the subject "Delete my uppr diet account", from the email address your account is registered with.
  2. We'll confirm the request and delete your account and all synced data within 30 days, then email you confirmation.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in credentials)
  • All data synced to your account: profile (height, weight, goals, preferences), food and drink logs, recipes, weight history, body measurements, fasting sessions, water/sleep/caffeine/alcohol logs, medication records and lab results

What is kept

  • Nothing personal. Anonymous contributions to the community food database (a product's name, brand, barcode and nutrition facts) are retained because they contain no personal information and are not linked to your account.
  • We may retain minimal records where required by law (e.g. evidence of a deletion request).

Deleting app data without deleting your account

In the app, Profile → "Reset all data" erases everything stored on your device at any time — no account or request needed. You can also use Settings → Export first to keep a copy.

Don't have an account?

If you never created an account, we hold no data about you — everything lives only on your device, and "Reset all data" removes it completely.

See also our Privacy Policy.
