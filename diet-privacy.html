Privacy Policy — uppr diet

Last updated: 2 July 2026

The short version

uppr diet works fully offline. Everything you log lives on your phone. If you choose to create an account, your data syncs to our servers so you can restore it on a new device — and that's the only reason we hold it. We don't sell data, we don't share it with third parties, and we don't run ads.

Data stored on your device

All app data is stored locally on your phone, including:

  • Food and drink logs, recipes, favourites and nutrition targets
  • Body weight, body measurements and progress photos
  • Fasting sessions, water, sleep, caffeine and alcohol logs
  • Medication tracking you set up (medication name, dose schedule, dose log, injection sites) and lab results you enter (e.g. IGF-1, HbA1c)
  • Health data you allow the app to read via Health Connect (Android) — weight and exercise sessions

Progress photos never leave your device. You can erase everything at any time with "Reset all data" in the app.

Data we collect if you create an account (optional)

  • Email address — for authentication and account recovery
  • Your profile (height, weight, sex, year of birth, goals, diet preferences) and the logs listed above (excluding photos, which stay on-device) — so we can restore them when you sign in on another device

Synced data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on servers we operate. Health-related data — including medication and lab entries — is used solely to provide the app's features to you. We never use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and we never disclose it to third parties except where required by law.

Community food database

If you add a new food (for example after scanning an unknown barcode), the food's nutrition facts — name, brand, barcode, calories and macros — may be shared to our community food database so other users can find it. This contains no personal information: nothing about you, your account, or what you ate is included.

Medication features

Medication tracking is a personal record-keeping tool. The app never recommends, calculates or adjusts medication doses — it only reminds you of, and records, the plan you enter from your prescriber. Nothing in the app is medical advice.

Third-party lookups

When you search for food or scan a barcode, the query (the search text or barcode number — never your identity) may be sent to public nutrition databases such as Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central to fetch nutrition facts.

Your rights

  • Access & portability: export your data as CSV/PDF from Settings → Export.
  • Deletion: delete your account and synced data via the steps at uppr.fit/diet-delete-account.html, and wipe local data with "Reset all data" in the app.
  • No account required: every feature works without signing in.

Children

uppr diet is designed for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll note it here with a new "last updated" date.

Contact
